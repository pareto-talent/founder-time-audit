# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-15)

First public version.

- Reads two weeks of calendar, gives a first read after two questions, then builds the full page.
- Scores ten time-management laws as On track, Drifting, Broken, or Can't tell yet.
- Works from the calendar alone. Sent email and call recordings can be added in a second pass.
- Clear rules for repeating, declined, free and all-day events, calendar-only runs, founders with no EA, and Buyback Rates under $25.
